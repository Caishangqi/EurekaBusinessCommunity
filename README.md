## About EurekaBusiness

EurekaBusiness is a collaborative business simulation and shop management mod for Minecraft. Build a physical shop in the world, display the goods you have collected or produced, set public prices, and turn clear business decisions into traceable income.

### Core Gameplay Loop

```text
Gather stock -> Display goods -> Set prices -> Serve customers -> Review results -> Expand
```

- **Build a Visible Shop**: Construct your own shop using Key Devices, Display Pedestals, Cash Registers, and shop boundaries instead of abstract menus.
- **Dynamic Elements System**: 48 distinct elemental aspects attached to merchandise, shaping item valuations and customer preferences.
- **Living Customer NPCs**: Customers walk into your store, inspect merchandise, express thoughts via speech bubbles, and queue up at the checkout counter.
- **Cheapest-First Selection**: When multiple pedestals offer identical items, customers intelligently prioritize the lowest-priced shelf.
- **Multiplayer & Server Authoritative**: Atomic transactions, secure validations, and robust persistence ensure zero duplicated items or desyncs.

## Compatibility & Platform

- **Minecraft**: `1.21.1`
- **Mod Loader**: `NeoForge 21.1.238`+
- **Java**: `Java 21`
- **Dependencies**: `Fzzy Config`, `Kotlin for Forge`
- **Unsupported**: Fabric is not supported.
